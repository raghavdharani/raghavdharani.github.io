# Portfolio design system

Approved direction: Data publication, selected by Raghav on 2026-10-10.

The primary reader evaluates product leadership through specific professional work, decisions and approved outcomes. Lead with role and scope, then selected work. The homepage is an Experience surface; the case study is Read.

Use warm white (#faf9f5), charcoal (#242421), secondary ink (#62615a), thin rules (#d5d3ca), and restrained brown link/focus emphasis (#795039). No forest-green feature panels or mint decoration. Georgia serves as the editorial headline voice with Times New Roman fallback; Helvetica Neue/Arial serves functional and body typography. Headlines use at most -0.035em tracking; body text is 17–19px with 1.65–1.75 line height. Labels are at least 12px and outcome evidence is 14px.

The grid has a 1240px outer boundary, fluid margins and a 210px employer rail for work entries. At narrow widths employer metadata moves above its narrative. Navigation remains visible. Thin rules separate evidence and sections; avoid repetitive card containers. Keep professional work separate from the private prototype and keep development status explicit.

Links use underlines or clear navigation placement, hover color and visible focus outlines. Content works without JavaScript. Reduced-motion preference disables smooth scrolling. No invented claims, confidential customer content, new imagery or public resume PDF.

Verification limitation: no browser captures were available in this environment. Source and generated HTML checks do not establish visual quality or absence of rendered overflow.
