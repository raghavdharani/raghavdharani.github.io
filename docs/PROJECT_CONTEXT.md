# Project context

## Purpose

This repository is Raghav Dharani's public product leadership portfolio. It demonstrates the scope, judgment, operating approach, and verified outcomes behind products he has built across enterprise SaaS, retail technology, merchandising, store operations, claims workflows, and intelligent automation.

The portfolio should establish senior product leadership through the quality of the work presented. It should not infer or publish private career motivations or tell visitors what conclusion to reach.

## Target audience

The intended readers are product, engineering, design, and business leaders evaluating senior product work. More specific career targeting discussed outside the repository is private context and should not be reproduced in public files or page copy.

## Positioning

The central positioning is:

> Product leader building enterprise products for work that cannot afford to break.

Supporting themes already established on the site:

- 10+ years turning operational complexity into scalable products
- Enterprise SaaS and workflow-heavy platforms
- Retail, merchandising, store operations, financial workflows, and intelligent automation
- Product leadership across discovery, workflow design, prototypes, specifications, platform decisions, rollout, and adoption
- AI used to improve judgment, structure ambiguous work, and validate decisions rather than to replace product thinking
- Comfort with incomplete signals, multiple systems, difficult stakeholders, and operational constraints

## Content strategy

The portfolio leads with evidence rather than a chronological resume.

Current content hierarchy:

1. A concise positioning statement and career summary
2. A proof strip with selected scale indicators
3. Four outcome-led professional case studies
4. Product operating principles
5. Evidence of prototype-led delivery
6. A short background section
7. Direct contact, LinkedIn, and GitHub links

Each case study uses the same compact structure:

- Situation: the operational problem and relevant constraints
- What I led: Raghav's direct responsibility
- Outcomes: a small set of verified measures

Current professional examples:

- KWI: merchandise transfer workflow modernization
- Upshop: mobile grocery store operations platform
- 360insights: enterprise claims platform rebuild
- EdgeVerve: intelligent automation product development

Case studies are intentionally summarized. Deeper studies may be added after public-safe narratives and supporting artifacts are reviewed.

## Public claim guardrails

Use only claims already present in the public site or newly verified by Raghav. The following figures are currently published, but this documentation review did not independently re-confirm them:

- KWI: 70% client migration within two months and 30% fewer transfer errors and exceptions
- Upshop: rollout across 100+ locations, 18% less overproduction, 40% higher engagement, and 12% lower inventory shrink
- 360insights: 100+ enterprise clients, doubled processing throughput, 50% less manual review, and 35% faster onboarding
- EdgeVerve: 50% higher platform adoption and 2017 Forrester Wave Strong Performer recognition
- Prototype-led delivery: approximately 25% fewer clarification cycles and 20 to 30% less rework

Do not introduce additional numbers without explicit verification. Do not publish customer names, confidential employer or customer material, proprietary workflows, or personal information that Raghav has not explicitly approved for public use.

## Design direction

The intended style is editorial, restrained, and confident rather than a conventional template portfolio.

Established visual language:

- Warm paper background: `#f2efe8`
- Deep forest green: `#152925`
- Burnt orange accent: `#e55d37`
- Pale mint accent: `#bad5c9`
- DM Sans for functional typography
- Instrument Serif for selected editorial emphasis
- Large asymmetric headlines, strong whitespace, thin dividing rules, compact labels, and simple process-line motifs

The site should feel designed by a thoughtful product leader, not generated from a stock template. Avoid badge walls, random quotes, GitHub vanity widgets, excessive emojis, neon gradients, generic card grids, fake user interfaces, laptop mockups, and decorative imagery without a clear purpose.

## Experience and interaction constraints

- The public site must remain responsive across desktop and mobile layouts.
- Navigation should be clear and lightweight.
- The page should work without JavaScript.
- Semantic structure, keyboard accessibility, readable contrast, and reduced-motion preferences should be preserved.
- The social preview and metadata should remain consistent with the current positioning.
- Copy should be natural, specific, and concise. Avoid em dashes and recognizable stock AI phrasing.
