# Project context

## Purpose

This repository is Raghav Dharani's public AI product leadership portfolio. It demonstrates the scope, judgment, operating approach, and verified outcomes behind products he has built across enterprise SaaS, retail technology, merchandising, store operations, claims workflows, and intelligent automation.

The portfolio should establish senior product leadership through evidence. It should not infer or publish private career motivations or tell visitors what conclusion to reach.

## Target audience

The intended readers are product, engineering, design, and business leaders evaluating senior applied-AI and enterprise product work. More specific career targeting discussed outside the repository is private context and should not be reproduced in public files.

## Positioning

The central positioning is:

> AI product leader turning complex workflows into intelligent products people can trust.

Supporting themes:

- 10+ years turning operational complexity into scalable products
- Applied AI at the product and workflow layer, without implying ML-infrastructure or engineering ownership
- Enterprise SaaS and workflow-heavy platforms
- Retail, merchandising, store operations, financial workflows, and intelligent automation
- Product leadership across discovery, workflow design, prototypes, specifications, platform decisions, rollout, and adoption
- AI used to improve judgment, structure ambiguous work, and validate decisions rather than replace product thinking

## Content strategy

The portfolio leads with evidence rather than a chronological résumé.

Current hierarchy:

1. Broad AI Product Leader positioning and recruiter actions
2. Selected operating scale with contextual labels
3. Five examples ordered for recency and relevance
4. Practical applied-AI evidence
5. Product operating principles
6. Background and direct email contact

Current examples:

- KWI: merchandising modernization and AI-assisted product delivery, explicitly labeled in development
- Upshop: ML-informed ordering and mobile grocery operations
- Keystone: private AI-assisted specification prototype, explicitly separated from professional production work
- EdgeVerve: intelligent automation product development
- 360insights: enterprise claims platform rebuild and product-team leadership

The KWI decision record is the first deeper case study. It describes constraints, trade-offs, validation, and the success model without claiming launch outcomes.

## Public claim guardrails

Use only claims present in the current approved résumé or newly verified by Raghav.

- KWI: five product domains, four legacy transfer workflows being unified, and purchase-order workflows supporting up to 20,000 lines. Do not claim Store Transfers migration or error-reduction outcomes while the product is in development.
- Upshop: rollout scope across 100+ locations, 18% less overproduction, and 40% higher engagement.
- 360insights: 100+ enterprise clients, doubled processing throughput, 25% less manual review effort, and 35% shorter enterprise implementations.
- EdgeVerve: 2017 Forrester Wave Strong Performer recognition. Do not publish the older 50% adoption claim without new confirmation.

Do not publish customer names, confidential employer or customer material, proprietary workflows, unsupported claims, or personal information Raghav has not approved.

## Design direction

Raghav selected Data publication on 2026-10-10: neutral warm white, charcoal, serif headlines, thin rules and restrained emphasis. The former forest-green, orange and mint system is superseded for the Astro redesign. `DESIGN.md` and `src/styles/global.css` define the current tokens. Use readable labels and evidence rather than oversized slogans, decorative numbering or quotation panels.

## Experience and publishing constraints

- Remain responsive and usable without JavaScript.
- Keep the résumé out of the public repository. Use the existing direct email contact links, as recorded in the 2026-10-10 editorial rebuild decision.
- Preserve semantic structure, keyboard accessibility, readable contrast, and reduced-motion support.
- Keep the social preview and metadata consistent with the visible positioning.
- Keep copy natural, specific, concise, and free of stock AI phrasing.
- Publish from the root of `main` at `https://raghavdharani.github.io/`.
- Use the existing Astro 5 and Tailwind CSS 4 static build, as explicitly requested by Raghav.
