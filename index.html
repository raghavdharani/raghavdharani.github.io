Applied AI in practice

Useful AI begins with a decision worth improving.

I work at the product layer: framing the problem, designing the human workflow, defining how outputs are reviewed, and deciding what success should mean.

01

Recommendations with override paths

At Upshop, I translated ML forecasts into ordering and production decisions with business rules, exceptions, feedback loops, and operational KPIs.

02

Automation with human review

At EdgeVerve, I defined how model outputs entered routing, queues, exception handling, monitoring, and human review.

03

AI-assisted product delivery

At KWI and in Keystone, I use AI tools to test workflows, surface ambiguity, prototype interactions, and improve requirements before engineering investment.

How I work

Make the important decisions visible.

My role is to make a difficult decision easier to understand, test, and act on without removing the complexity that matters.

01

Start with operating reality

I spend time with the people doing the work, map exceptions early, and design for real conditions rather than only the happy path.

02

Separate signal from assumption

I make evidence, open questions, and trade-offs explicit so teams know what is validated and what still needs testing.

03

Prototype the decision

Working prototypes let users and delivery teams test the workflow before expensive choices become difficult to reverse.

04

Plan for adoption

Rollout, feedback, operational measures, and migration constraints belong in the product plan from the start.
