How I work

Clarity is a product advantage.

My job is to make a difficult decision easier to understand, test, and act on without sanding away the complexity that matters.

01

Start with operating reality

I spend time with the people doing the work, map exceptions early, and design for the conditions that exist, not the happy path.

02

Make decisions inspectable

Clear problem framing, explicit trade-offs, and written decisions keep teams aligned when the answer is not obvious.

03

Prototype before the handoff

Working prototypes and detailed specs let stakeholders test a workflow before engineering commits to the build.

04

Treat adoption as product work

Rollout design, feedback loops, and operational measures belong inside the product plan, not after launch.

Prototype-led delivery

≈25%fewer clarification cycles
20–30%less rework

I use prototypes and implementation-ready specs to bring live stakeholder validation forward, before the expensive decisions are locked.
